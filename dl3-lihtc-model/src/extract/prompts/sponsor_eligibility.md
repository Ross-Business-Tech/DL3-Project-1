fields: [experience_requirement, capacity_requirement, new_developer_path, joint_venture_rule, disqualification_rule, other_eligibility]
---
Extract who may apply as developer or sponsor.

- experience_requirement: required prior experience (number of LIHTC projects, units, years, placed-in-service).
- capacity_requirement: financial capacity, net worth, liquidity, or staffing thresholds.
- new_developer_path: how a developer without enough experience can qualify (for example by partnering).
- joint_venture_rule: rules for co-developers or joint ventures (ownership share, fee split, roles).
- disqualification_rule: conditions that make a developer ineligible (noncompliance, debarment, past defaults).
- other_eligibility: any other sponsor or developer eligibility rule.

Extract limits on project cost.

- tdc_per_unit_limit: any maximum total development cost per unit (or per bedroom, or per square foot).
  One finding per distinct limit; say in notes which project type, building type, or area it applies to.
- cost_limit_basis: exactly what the limit is measured against (which costs are included or excluded,
  for example whether land, reserves, or developer fee are excluded).
- cost_limit_by_type: differences in the limit by construction type, building type, unit size, or location.
- hard_cost_limit: limits on construction (hard) costs, contractor profit, overhead, or general requirements.
- cost_limit_exception: waivers or exceptions to the cost limits, and who grants them.
- other_cost_limit: any other cost containment rule (state it in notes).

Give amounts exactly as printed. Do not compute a per-unit figure the document does not state.

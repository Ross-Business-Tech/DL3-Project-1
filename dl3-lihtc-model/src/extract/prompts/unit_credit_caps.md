Extract per-project and per-sponsor limits on credits and size.

- max_annual_credit_per_project: the most annual credit one project may receive.
- max_credit_per_unit: any per-unit or per-bedroom credit or cost limit.
- max_units_per_project: any maximum number of units (including limits for particular set-asides or areas).
- min_units_per_project: any minimum project size.
- credit_cap_per_sponsor: limits on credits or applications per developer, sponsor, or principal per round.
- other_cap: any other cap that limits project scale or credit amount (state the cap's subject in notes).

Keep annual credit amounts distinct from ten-year totals: if the document gives a ten-year figure, say so in notes.

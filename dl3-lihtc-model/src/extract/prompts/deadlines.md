Extract application dates for the funding round(s) the document covers.

- application_deadline: the full application due date (and time, if given) for each round.
- preliminary_application_deadline: any pre-application, intent-to-apply, or site-review deadline.
- round_name: the name or year of each round the dates belong to.
- award_announcement_date: when awards are expected to be announced.
- other_deadline: other dates a developer must meet before award (state what it is in notes).

Give dates exactly as printed. If the document says dates will be announced later, report that as the value.

Extract how much credit is available and how the 4% tax-exempt bond pathway works.

- annual_credit_ceiling: the state's annual 9% credit ceiling or per-capita amount, with the year it is for.
- credits_available_this_round: the amount of credit stated as available for the round(s) covered.
- bond_volume_cap: the private activity bond volume cap, or the amount set aside for multifamily housing.
- bond_issuer: each entity named as issuing tax-exempt bonds for 4% projects (one finding each).
- four_percent_process: how a 4% application is made and decided (rolling or competitive, who approves).
- four_percent_limit: caps, scoring, or eligibility limits that apply specifically to 4% bond projects.
- other_availability: other statements about how much funding exists or how it is divided.

Report annual amounts as annual. If a figure is a ten-year total, a per-capita rate, or an estimate, say so in notes.

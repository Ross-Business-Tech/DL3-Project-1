---
fields: [set_aside, geographic_pool, nonprofit_set_aside, other_set_aside]
---
Extract the set-asides and funding pools a project competes in.

- set_aside: each named set-aside with its share or amount of the credit ceiling (one finding each).
- geographic_pool: geographic pools or regions (urban, rural, named cities or regions) with their share.
- nonprofit_set_aside: the qualified nonprofit set-aside.
- other_set_aside: any other reserved pool (for example supportive housing, preservation, 4% bond deals).
